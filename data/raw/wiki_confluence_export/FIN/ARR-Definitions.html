FIN : ARR Definitions (read before quoting ARR)

Created by Lorraine Whitlock on Jan 22, 2020, last modified by Ines Delacroix on Sep 03, 2021

We report ARR two ways and they will not match. Both are correct for their purpose.

Live ARR (Finance)

Annualised MRR of subscriptions that are live (service started, not cancelled) on the last day of the period, converted to USD at the planning rates (CAD 0.75, GBP 1.27). Source of truth: billing system + GL. This is the number in the finance close workbook.

Contracted ARR (Board / Sales)

Live ARR plus first-year ARR of contracts signed on or before period end whose service starts after period end ("signed-not-started"). Pulled from Salesforce opportunities (Closed Won, CloseDate <= period end, Contract_Start__c > period end). Used in board decks since Q3 2018.

What is NOT ARR

  • Classic maintenance (on-prem) - reported separately until the June 2020 sunset
  • One-off services, training
  • Salesforce Account.ARR__c - this field is set at close and rarely updated. Do not sum it.
