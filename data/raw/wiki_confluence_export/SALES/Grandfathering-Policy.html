SALES : Price increases and grandfathering

Created by Renata Esposito on Jan 05, 2023, last modified by Renata Esposito on Apr 15, 2023

When list prices change (2015, 2019, 2023) existing customers keep the per-seat price they signed at until they re-price (plan change or a negotiated renewal). New seats added on an existing plan get the customer's grandfathered price.

Exception: Kestrel/Insights customers were moved to the 2023 Insights flat fee.
