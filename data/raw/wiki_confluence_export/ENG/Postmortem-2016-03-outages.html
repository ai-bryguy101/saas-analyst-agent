ENG : Postmortem: March 2016 outages (03-08, 03-22)

Created by Desmond Fairweather on Mar 25, 2016, last modified by Desmond Fairweather on Jul 01, 2016

Two multi-hour outages in March 2016 caused by the MyISAM table locks on the Ledger/API shared DB during month-start invoice runs.

03-08: 13:05-16:50 ET. 03-22: 09:40-11:15 ET. Several enterprise customers escalated (see helpdesk tag outage).

Fix: split billing DB from app DB (done 2016-05).
