ENG : Postmortem: 2017-11-05 DB connection pool exhaustion

Created by Callum MacLeod on Nov 06, 2017, last modified by Callum MacLeod on Feb 12, 2018

Summary

Early Sunday Nov 5 the API could not get database connections. Customers could not load or sync work orders.

Impact

~40 minutes of full outage (01:20 - 02:00 per app03 logs). Low traffic window, limited customer impact.

Root cause

A long-running PM schedule backfill held connections; pool max=50. Nightly vacuum ran at the same time.

Action items

  • Raise pool to 120 (done)
  • Move backfill to replica (ENG-1102)
  • Logs in UTC (ENG-1180)
